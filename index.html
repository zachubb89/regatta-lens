Markers

Each piece wears one square. The top edge is the boat's bow, the wind arrow's upwind end, and the course base's "toward the finish" side.

How it works

Regatta Lens reads small black-and-white squares on your pieces, works out where every boat sits on the table, and draws your legal moves on top: how far each card takes you, who you'd steal wind from, who has right of way, and where the laylines are.

Set up once

  1. Print the marker sheet at 100% (Settings → Save printable markers). Matte paper; gloss glares.
  2. Wind marker on the dial's rotating arrow hub, top edge upwind. Course marker on the fixed base, top edge toward the finish.
  3. One boat marker flat on each meeple, top edge = bow. Course-mark markers on top of the cylinders.

On your turn

  1. Photo: snap the table from above. Live: point the camera at it.
  2. Tap your boat. Tap a move to see the cards laid out and what it costs you.

Reading the overlay

Cards show each Sail move along your close-hauled line. Brown boxes are wind shadows (sail through or end in one and you draw Dirty Air). Green dashed boxes are the area a starboard boat controls. Red/green dashed lines are the port and starboard laylines to the finish. The dark wedge is the no-sail zone.
